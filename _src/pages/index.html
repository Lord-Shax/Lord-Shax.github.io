My vision · three pillars of AI safety

A world of abundance and safety

I am Ishaku. I care about satellite technology and AI. I want to see a world without disease, with abundant healthy food, and with a strong relationship of trust, safety and security between AI and humans.

Three pillars, each told through a story far older than computers.

Safety

The two faces of Janus

What we ask for must match exactly what we deserve, with no undesirable add-ons embedded maliciously.

Trust

The night shift

When no one is watching, will AI remain trustworthy?

Read the parable

In Plato’s Republic, the Ring of Gyges makes its wearer invisible, and asks whether anyone stays good when unseen. A trustworthy AI does the same careful work in the dark as under the brightest lights.

Responsibility

Ubuntu: I am because we are

AI should warm the whole circle, and never break it.

Read the parable

The Nguni saying umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu means a person is a person through other people. Deploy AI like the fire at the centre of the circle: shared and tended together, pluralistic and community-centred, never robotic or profit-first.
